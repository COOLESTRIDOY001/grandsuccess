About Us

Building Future Leaders THrough Excellence, Discipline and Knowledge

Our School History

Grand Success School was established in 2012 with the vision of providing quality primary & secondary education that nurtures academic excellence, strong moral values and leadership skills. Over the years, we have grown into a reputable institution known for producing well-rounded and responsible young individuals.

Our School Purpose

The purpose of Grand Success School is to provide a holistic education that empowers students to become lifelong learners, critical thinkers, and responsible citizens. We aim to create a nurturing environment where students can explore their interests, develop their talents, and achieve their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that develops the whole child academically, socially,emotionally and morally.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that fosters innovation, integrity, and excellence. Also, to be able to produce future leders and responsible citizens

Our Core Values

Discipline │ Integrity │ Excellence │ Respect │ Teamwork

Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. - Nelson Mandela

At Grand Success School, we believe that education is the key to unlocking a brighter future. We are committed to providing our students with the knowledge, skills, and values they need to make a positive impact on the world.
